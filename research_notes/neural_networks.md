# Neural Networks

## Why Neural Networks?

A neural network can learn patterns from data instead of relying only on manually written rules.

For chess, a neural network could learn to evaluate a chess position or predict a move.

## Basic Idea

Chess board
↓
Numerical representation
↓
Neural network
↓
Prediction

## Chess Board Representation

A chess board must be converted into numerical data before it can be given to a neural network.

One possible representation is an 8 × 8 × 12 tensor.

The 12 layers can represent:
- White pawn
- White knight
- White bishop
- White rook
- White queen
- White king
- Black pawn
- Black knight
- Black bishop
- Black rook
- Black queen
- Black king
